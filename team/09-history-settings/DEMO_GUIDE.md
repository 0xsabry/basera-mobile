# 09 — Demo Guide: History & Settings

## Live demo (45 seconds)

1. Run one detection, save it, open the History tab, replay it (it speaks
   the stored description), then delete the item.
2. In Settings: flip the theme to dark and back; switch language to
   English and back to Arabic — show the instant recolor and the RTL flip.
3. Show the voice preference row leading to the voice settings screen.

## Expected behavior

- The saved item survives leaving and re-entering the tab.
- Theme and language changes apply without restarting the app manually.
- History never exceeds 50 items (oldest are dropped automatically).

## Fallback

- If the room is short on time, skip the live save: the History tab from
  earlier demos already holds items — replay one of those.
- Do not factory-reset or clear data to "clean up" before the defense;
  the stored items are your fallback for sections 01/04/07.

## Before the session

- Seed the history with 2–3 good results from the sample images.
- Leave settings at: Arabic or English (your presenting language), light
  theme, announcements on.
